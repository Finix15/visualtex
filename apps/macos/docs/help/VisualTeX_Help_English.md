# VisualTeX Help Manual

**Version: macOS 1.2.6**

## Quick start

1. Enter or paste LaTeX in a formula field.
2. Use Formula Tools to insert structures and symbols.
3. Copy LaTeX, export an image, or insert the formula into Word or PowerPoint.

## Formula layout

- **Inline formula** places a formula within a line of text.
- **Display formula** places a formula on its own line.
- Use the toolbar to align, resize, number, or redraw formulas.

## OCR

VisualTeX includes the M OCR model. The S and L models are optional. Paste or capture a clear formula image, review the result, and then insert it into the editor.

## Microsoft Office

Install the Word DOTM and PowerPoint PPAM from Settings. The Office Ribbon follows the app language after Word or PowerPoint is closed and reopened. Missing or damaged language preferences fall back to English.

Word supports inline and display formulas, equation numbering, cross-references, conversion between image formulas and OMML, document import, and LaTeX redraw.

PowerPoint supports creating, editing, deleting, and resizing VisualTeX formulas.

## Legacy documents

Documents created by earlier releases remain readable. Unicode content and legacy metadata are preserved. The former Chinese locale preference opens in English.

## Troubleshooting

- Restart VisualTeX after changing OCR models.
- Restart Word or PowerPoint after changing the app language.
- If an Office add-in is unavailable, reinstall it from Settings and reopen Office.
- Internal MathLive menus use English when Vietnamese localization is unavailable.
